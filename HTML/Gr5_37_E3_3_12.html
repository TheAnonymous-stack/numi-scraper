Choosing the Best Graph Type for Netflix Episodes Data

Pictograph

Perfect for data in groups!

Family A: ● ● ● = 30 netflix episodes
Family B: ● ● = 20 netflix episodes
Family C: ● ● ● ● = 40 netflix episodes
● = 10 netflix episodes (1 season)

Shows seasons of 10 clearly!

Bar Graph

Shows individual values

Family A:
30
Family B:
20
Family C:
40

Doesn't show season groupings

Line Graph

Shows change over time

Not for comparing categories

Key Point: When netflix episodes come in seasons of 10, a pictograph makes the groupings visual and easy to understand!

Choosing the Best Graph Type for Apple Airpods Data

Pictograph

Perfect for data in groups!

Store A: ● ● ● ● ● = 10 apple airpods
Store B: ● ● ● = 6 apple airpods
Store C: ● ● ● ● ● ● ● = 14 apple airpods
● = 2 apple airpods (1 pair)

Shows pairs of 2 clearly!

Bar Graph

Shows individual values

Store A:
10
Store B:
6
Store C:
14

Doesn't show pair groupings

Line Graph

Shows change over time

Not for comparing categories

Key Point: When apple airpods come in pairs of 2, a pictograph makes the groupings visual and easy to understand!

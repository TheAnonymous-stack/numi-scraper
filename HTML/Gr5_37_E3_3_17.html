Choosing the Best Graph Type for Tesla Charging Stations Data

Pictograph

Perfect for data in groups!

Location A: ● ● ● = 12 tesla charging stations
Location B: ● ● = 8 tesla charging stations
Location C: ● ● ● ● = 16 tesla charging stations
● = 4 tesla charging stations (1 cluster)

Shows clusters of 4 clearly!

Bar Graph

Shows individual values

Location A:
12
Location B:
8
Location C:
16

Doesn't show cluster groupings

Line Graph

Shows change over time

Not for comparing categories

Key Point: When tesla charging stations come in clusters of 4, a pictograph makes the groupings visual and easy to understand!

Grade 7 - Week 20, Exercise 3

Question 3 (Variation 29):
A recipe for Stranger Things portals uses $11/2$ cups of flour. Another recipe uses $5/4$ cups. What's the total? Express your answer in simplest form.
Answer:
27/4

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
We need to find the total amount of flour for both recipes.
Step 2:
We have $\frac{11}{2}$ cups from the first recipe and $\frac{5}{4}$ cups from the second recipe.\nTo add these, we need a common denominator of 4:\n$\frac{11}{2} = \frac{22}{4}$ and $\frac{5}{4} = \frac{5}{4}$
Step 3:
Adding the fractions:\n$\frac{22}{4} + \frac{5}{4} = \frac{27}{4}$
Step 4:
The total amount of flour needed for Zoom meetings is $\frac{27}{4}$ cups.\nAs a mixed number: $6\frac{3}{4}$ cups.

Question 2_50

Which number line models 6 - 5?

Solution

To model 6 - 5 on a number line, first rewrite the expression using addition. 6 - 5 = 6 + (-5)

Since the expression is the same as 6 + (-5), you can start at 6 on the number line and move in the negative direction 5 units.

To find the missing number, see where the arrow ends. The arrow ends at 1, so 6 + (-5) = 1. So 6 - 5 = 1.

Choosing the Best Graph Type for Spotify Songs Data

Pictograph

Perfect for data in groups!

Playlist A: ● ● ● = 75 spotify songs
Playlist B: ● ● ● ● = 100 spotify songs
Playlist C: ● ● = 50 spotify songs
● = 25 spotify songs (1 set)

Shows sets of 25 clearly!

Bar Graph

Shows individual values

Playlist A:
75
Playlist B:
100
Playlist C:
50

Doesn't show set groupings

Line Graph

Shows change over time

Not for comparing categories

Key Point: When spotify songs come in sets of 25, a pictograph makes the groupings visual and easy to understand!

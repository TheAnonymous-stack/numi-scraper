Choosing the Best Graph Type for Roblox Robux Data

Pictograph

Perfect for data in groups!

Player A: ● ● ● = 1200 roblox robux
Player B: ● ● = 800 roblox robux
Player C: ● ● ● ● = 1600 roblox robux
● = 400 roblox robux (1 package)

Shows packages of 400 clearly!

Bar Graph

Shows individual values

Player A:
1200
Player B:
800
Player C:
1600

Doesn't show package groupings

Line Graph

Shows change over time

Not for comparing categories

Key Point: When roblox robux come in packages of 400, a pictograph makes the groupings visual and easy to understand!

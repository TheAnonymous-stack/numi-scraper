Grade 7 - Week 18, Exercise 3

Question 3 (Variation 3):
In preparation for a classroom, Emily is setting up some stations where people can organize materials. She has 19 notebooks and 10 pencils, which she wants to distribute evenly among the stations with none left over. What is the greatest number of stations that Emily can set up? . Express your answer as a ratio in simplest form.
Answer:
1

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
Emily wants to divide 19 notebooks and 10 pencils into equal groups with no extras.
Step 2:
We need to find the greatest number that can divide both 19 and 10 evenly.
Step 3:
The factors of 19 are: 1, 19.
Step 4:
The factors of 10 are: 1, 2, 5, 10.
Step 5:
The greatest common factor is 1. That means Emily can make 1 stations.
Step 6:
Each station would have 19 notebooks and 10 pencils. So, the greatest number of stations is 1.

Question 1 (Variation 2):
The model below shows $\frac{1}{2} x \frac{4}{5}$. Use the model to solve $\frac{1}{2} x \frac{4}{5}$ = _ (answer in simplest form)
Answer:
2/5

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
To model $\frac{1}{2} x \frac{4}{5}$, it can be helpful to think of the product as $\frac{1}{2}$ of $\frac{4}{5}$. Start with a model of $\frac{4}{5}$. Divide a square into 5 columns, or fifths. Then shade 4 of the columns.
Step 2:
To show $\frac{1}{2}$ of $\frac{4}{5}$, divide the square into 2 rows, or halves. Then shade 1 of the shaded rows again. The area you shaded twice shows $\frac{1}{2}$ of $\frac{4}{5}$
Step 3:
The model shows 10 equal parts, and 4 of them are shaded twice. This means $\frac{1}{2}$ of $\frac{4}{5}$ is equal to $\frac{4}{10}$ of the whole.
Step 4:
We can simplify the solution further: $\frac{4}{10}$ = $\frac{2}{5}$.

Choosing the Best Graph Type for Youtube Subscribers Data

Pictograph

Perfect for data in groups!

Channel A: ● ● ● ● ● = 500 youtube subscribers
Channel B: ● ● ● = 300 youtube subscribers
Channel C: ● ● ● ● ● ● ● = 700 youtube subscribers
● = 100 youtube subscribers (1 group)

Shows groups of 100 clearly!

Bar Graph

Shows individual values

Channel A:
500
Channel B:
300
Channel C:
700

Doesn't show group groupings

Line Graph

Shows change over time

Not for comparing categories

Key Point: When youtube subscribers come in groups of 100, a pictograph makes the groupings visual and easy to understand!

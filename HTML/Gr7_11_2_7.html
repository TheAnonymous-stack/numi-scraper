Question 2_7

Iron Man's suit power started at 4 and decreased by 12 after flying. Which number line models 4 - 12?

Solution

To model 4 - 12 on a number line, first rewrite the expression using addition. 4 - 12 = 4 + (-12)

Since the expression is the same as 4 + (-12), you can start at 4 on the number line and move in the negative direction 12 units.

To find the missing number, see where the arrow ends. The arrow ends at -8, so 4 + (-12) = -8. So 4 - 12 = -8.

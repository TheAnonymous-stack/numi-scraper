Question 2_22

Which number line models 1 - 9?

Solution

To model 1 - 9 on a number line, first rewrite the expression using addition. 1 - 9 = 1 + (-9)

Since the expression is the same as 1 + (-9), you can start at 1 on the number line and move in the negative direction 9 units.

To find the missing number, see where the arrow ends. The arrow ends at -8, so 1 + (-9) = -8. So 1 - 9 = -8.

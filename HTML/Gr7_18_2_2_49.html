Grade 7 - Week 18, Exercise 2

Question 2 (Variation 49):
What is the least common multiple of 9 and 15? . Simplify your answer.
Answer:
45

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
To find the least common multiple (LCM) of 9 and 15, list the multiples of each number.
Step 2:
Multiples of 9: 9, 18, 27, 36, ...
Step 3:
Multiples of 15: 15, 30, 45, 60, ...
Step 4:
The first multiple that both numbers share is 45.
Step 5:
So, the least common multiple of 9 and 15 is 45.

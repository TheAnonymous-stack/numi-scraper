Question 2 (Variation 19):
A recipe for YouTube subscribers uses $5/2$ cups of flour. Another recipe uses $9/2$ cups. What's the total? Express your answer in simplest form.
Answer:
7

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
To find how much gas Rita used, subtract the amount she had left from the amount she started with: $\frac{1}{2} − \frac{1}{6}$.
Step 2:
The least common denominator of 2 and 6 is 6. Rewrite $\frac{1}{2}$ as an equivalent fraction with a denominator of 6: $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{6}$.
Step 3:
Now subtract the fractions: $\frac{3}{6} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{6}$.
Step 4:
So, Rita used $\frac{2}{6}$ of a tank of gas. This can be simplified to $\frac{1}{3}$.

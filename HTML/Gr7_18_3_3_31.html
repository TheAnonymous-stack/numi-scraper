Grade 7 - Week 18, Exercise 3

Question 3 (Variation 31):
In preparation for a event, Carlos is setting up some stations where people can organize materials. He has 48 badges and 26 lanyards, which he wants to distribute evenly among the stations with none left over. What is the greatest number of stations that Carlos can set up? . Express your answer as a ratio in simplest form.
Answer:
2

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
Carlos wants to divide 48 badges and 26 lanyards into equal groups with no extras.
Step 2:
We need to find the greatest number that can divide both 48 and 26 evenly.
Step 3:
The factors of 48 are: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48.
Step 4:
The factors of 26 are: 1, 2, 13, 26.
Step 5:
The greatest common factor is 2. That means Carlos can make 2 stations.
Step 6:
Each station would have 24 badges and 13 lanyards. So, the greatest number of stations is 2.

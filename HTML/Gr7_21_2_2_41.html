Question 2 (Variation 41):
A recipe requires $3 rac{2}{3}$ cups of flour per batch. If you make $1 rac{2}{3}$ batches, how many cups of flour will you need in total? Express your answer as a mixed number.
Answer:
6 1/9

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
We need to multiply $3 rac{2}{3}$ × $1 rac{2}{3}$.
Step 2:
Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions: $3 rac{2}{3} = rac{11}{3}$ and $1 rac{2}{3} = rac{5}{3}$.
Step 3:
Multiply the fractions: $ rac{11}{3} × rac{5}{3} = rac{55}{9}$.
Step 4:
Convert to mixed number: $ rac{55}{9} = 6 1/9$.
Step 5:
You will need $6 1/9$ cups of flour.

Question 2_20

Soccer goals started at 7 and decreased by 1 after VAR review. Which number line models 7 - 1?

Solution

To model 7 - 1 on a number line, first rewrite the expression using addition. 7 - 1 = 7 + (-1)

Since the expression is the same as 7 + (-1), you can start at 7 on the number line and move in the negative direction 1 units.

To find the missing number, see where the arrow ends. The arrow ends at 6, so 7 + (-1) = 6. So 7 - 1 = 6.

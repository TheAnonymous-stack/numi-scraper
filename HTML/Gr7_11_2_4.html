Question 2_4

Pikachu's HP started at 1 and decreased by 5 after taking damage. Which number line models 1 - 5?

Solution

To model 1 - 5 on a number line, first rewrite the expression using addition. 1 - 5 = 1 + (-5)

Since the expression is the same as 1 + (-5), you can start at 1 on the number line and move in the negative direction 5 units.

To find the missing number, see where the arrow ends. The arrow ends at -4, so 1 + (-5) = -4. So 1 - 5 = -4.

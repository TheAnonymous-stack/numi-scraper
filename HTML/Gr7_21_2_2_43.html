Grade 7 - Week 21, Exercise 2

Question 2 (Variation 43):
A painting requires $1 rac{3}{4}$ gallons of paint per batch. If you need $1 rac{1}{5}$ batches, how many gallons of paint will you need in total? Express your answer as a mixed number.
Answer:
2 1/10

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
We need to multiply $1 rac{3}{4}$ × $1 rac{1}{5}$.
Step 2:
Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions: $1 rac{3}{4} = rac{7}{4}$ and $1 rac{1}{5} = rac{6}{5}$.
Step 3:
Multiply the fractions: $ rac{7}{4} × rac{6}{5} = rac{21}{10}$.
Step 4:
Convert to mixed number: $ rac{21}{10} = 2 1/10$.
Step 5:
You will need $2 1/10$ gallons of paint.

Grade 7 - Week 18, Exercise 3

Question 3 (Variation 50):
Over the next two days, Professional Employment Agency is interviewing clients who wish to find jobs. On the first day, the agency plans to interview clients in groups of 6. On the second day, the agency will interview clients in groups of 14. If the employment agency will interview the same number of clients on each day, what is the smallest number of clients that could be interviewed each day? . Simplify your answer.
Answer:
42

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
The agency plans to interview clients in groups of 6 on the first day and in groups of 14 on the second day.
Step 2:
We are told the number of clients interviewed each day must be the same. So, we need to find the smallest number that both 6 and 14 can divide into evenly.
Step 3:
The multiples of 6 are: 6, 12, 18, 24...
Step 4:
The multiples of 14 are: 14, 28, 42, 56...
Step 5:
The least common multiple (LCM) of 6 and 14 is 42. That means 42 is the smallest number of clients that works for both grouping methods.
Step 6:
So, the smallest number of clients that could be interviewed each day is 42.

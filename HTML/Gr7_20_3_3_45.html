Grade 7 - Week 20, Exercise 3

Question 3 (Variation 45):
A recipe for Star Wars lightsabers uses $11/5$ cups of flour. Another recipe uses $3/2$ cups. What's the total? Express your answer in simplest form.
Answer:
37/10

Solution Steps:

Step 1:
We need to find the total amount of flour for both recipes.
Step 2:
We have $\frac{11}{5}$ cups from the first recipe and $\frac{3}{2}$ cups from the second recipe.\nTo add these, we need a common denominator of 5:\n$\frac{11}{5} = \frac{11}{5}$ and $\frac{3}{2} = \frac{6}{5}$
Step 3:
Adding the fractions:\n$\frac{11}{5} + \frac{6}{5} = \frac{17}{5}$
Step 4:
The total amount of flour needed for Stock trades is $\frac{37}{10}$ cups.\nAs a mixed number: $3\frac{7}{10}$ cups.

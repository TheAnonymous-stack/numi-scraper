Question 15

Calculate: 33 + -27 = ?

A
B
C
D

Solution

Step 1/6: We are solving –1 + 4 using counters.

Step 2/6: Start with 1 negative counter to represent –1.

Step 3/6: Add 4 positive counters to represent +4.

Step 4/6: Pair 1 negative and 1 positive counter together to make a zero pair, then remove them.

Step 5/6: 3 positive counters remain.

Step 6/6: So -1 + 4 = 3. Option C is correct because it shows 3 positive counters.
